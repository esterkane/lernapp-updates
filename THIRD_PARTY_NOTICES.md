## Optional local German voice (explicit user installation)

Piper TTS 1.8.0 is an optional extra, not in the default installer dependencies.
Engine source and GPL-3.0 license: https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl .
Dependencies are resolved from the application's frozen uv.lock through the local-tts extra.
The model is downloaded from rhasspy/piper-voices at revision
1162a9173d0ce503555aed757976b7a9912eae4c, de/de_DE/thorsten/medium.
Its MODEL_CARD is retained beside the model and identifies the Thorsten dataset as CC0;
it also states that training was fine-tuned from the English lessac medium voice.
This dataset declaration is not a blanket claim that all optional components are MIT licensed.
The source model card is https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/1162a9173d0ce503555aed757976b7a9912eae4c/de/de_DE/thorsten/medium/MODEL_CARD .
